# ✈️ MakeMyTrip Travel Booking & Customer Intelligence Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes travel booking and customer data inspired by MakeMyTrip, a leading online travel booking platform. It uses Excel Power Query, SQL, and Power BI to transform raw booking data into meaningful business insights.

The project includes four interactive Power BI dashboards covering overall business performance, flight and hotel bookings, customer segmentation using RFM analysis, and cancellation and payment trends.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze booking volume, revenue, and booking trends.
- Compare flight and hotel booking performance.
- Segment customers using Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) analysis.
- Investigate booking cancellations and payment patterns.
- Develop interactive dashboards to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Excel Power Query** – Data cleaning, transformation, and validation
- **SQL** – Data analysis, aggregations, CTEs, and window functions
- **Power BI** – Interactive dashboards and visualizations
- **DAX** – KPI calculations, measures, and business metrics
- **Data Modeling** – Relationships and analytical model design

## 🔄 Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning & Transformation — Excel Power Query

- Handled missing values and inconsistent data formats.
- Standardized relevant booking categories and status fields.
- Validated dates, amounts, customer details, and booking records.
- Prepared structured datasets for SQL analysis and Power BI reporting.

### 2. Data Analysis — SQL

- Analyzed booking volume and revenue trends.
- Compared flight and hotel booking performance.
- Calculated customer-level metrics for RFM segmentation.
- Examined cancellation rates and payment status patterns.
- Used aggregations, CTEs, and window functions for deeper analysis.

### 3. Data Modeling & DAX — Power BI

- Designed relationships between relevant tables.
- Created DAX measures for revenue, bookings, cancellation rates, and other KPIs.
- Developed customer segmentation logic using RFM metrics.
- Implemented interactive slicers, filters, and cross-visual interactions.

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Summary
Provides an overview of travel booking performance and key business metrics.

**Key analysis areas:**
- Total bookings and booking revenue
- Booking trends over time
- Booking status distribution
- Booking channel performance
- Overall business performance indicators

### 2. Flight & Hotel Analysis
Compares flight and hotel booking performance to identify trends and differences.

**Key analysis areas:**
- Flight versus hotel bookings
- Revenue by booking category
- Booking trends over time
- Booking channel and location analysis
- Average booking value and category performance

### 3. Customer RFM Analysis
Segments customers according to their purchasing behavior using Recency, Frequency, and Monetary value.

**Key analysis areas:**
- Recency: Time since the customer's latest booking
- Frequency: Number of bookings made by the customer
- Monetary: Total booking value generated by the customer
- Customer segments such as Champions, Loyal, Potential Loyalists, At Risk, and Lost
- Customer distribution and revenue contribution by segment

### 4. Cancellation & Payment Analysis
Examines booking cancellations and payment outcomes to understand booking reliability and payment behavior.

**Key analysis areas:**
- Cancellation rate and cancellation trends
- Booking status distribution
- Payment status and payment method analysis
- Cancellation patterns by booking category and channel
- Revenue associated with successful and cancelled bookings, based on the available data

## 💡 Business Questions Answered

- How are booking volume and revenue changing over time?
- Which booking category contributes more revenue?
- Which customer segments generate the most booking value?
- How many customers fall into high-value or at-risk segments?
- What proportion of bookings are cancelled?
- Which payment methods and booking channels show different performance patterns?
